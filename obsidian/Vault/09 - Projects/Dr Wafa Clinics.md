# Dr Wafa Clinics

## Summary

Scalable healthcare/hospital management concept covering appointments,
pharmacy e-commerce, billing, procurement, scheduling, insurance,
inventory and role-based dashboards. Detail content also describes
security, query optimization and mobile workflows.

## Stack represented in source

Next.js, React Native, MUI, NestJS, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Docker,
Vercel.
